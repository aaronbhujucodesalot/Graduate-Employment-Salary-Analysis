# Dashboard Design: Graduate Employment & Salary Analysis

## Page 1: Executive Overview
### KPI Cards
- Highest median income field
- Highest employment rate field
- Average median income
- Average employment rate
- Highest further-study field

### Visuals
- Bar chart: Median income by field
- Scatter plot: Employment rate vs income
- Table: Top 5 fields by opportunity score

## Page 2: Income Analysis
### Visuals
- Bar chart: Median annual income by field and years after graduation
- Line chart: Income growth over time for selected fields
- Slicer: Field group

## Page 3: Employment Outcomes
### Visuals
- Bar chart: Employment rate by field
- Heatmap: Employment rate by years after graduation
- Table: Employment, further study, jobseeker and overseas rates

## Page 4: Risk and Pathway Analysis
### Visuals
- Scatter plot: Further-study rate vs employment rate
- Bar chart: Jobseeker rate by field
- Commentary box: Interpretation of fields with high further-study rates

## Page 5: Recommendations
### Recommended Insights
- Highlight fields with both high income and high employment.
- Explain fields where further study is common and immediate employment rates are lower.
- Avoid ranking courses only by salary; include employment rate and long-term income growth.
